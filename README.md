# Thesis #51: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR EMERGENT PROTEOFORM DISCOVERY IN COMPLEX PATHOLOGIES

**Author:** Kelechi Emeka Ogbonna
**Date:** September 2026
**Format:** Nile University B.Sc. project chapter structure (5 Chapters)

## Overview
Cancer is not merely a disease of mutated genes, but a complex adaptive system that produces emergent molecular states—proteoforms that do not exist in the canonical healthy proteome. This thesis establishes a complexity-theoretic framework for AI to predict and discover these emergent proteins, integrating multi-scale biological emergence (from molecular to systemic) and attractor theory.

This work builds upon the broader Project Confluence thesis series, heavily cross-referencing foundational frameworks such as the NSTG framework (Thesis T02), Disease Profile formulation (T03), and specific pathology models in TNBC (T07), circadian disruption (T46), the dark proteome (T47), and pre-metastatic niche formation (T49).
